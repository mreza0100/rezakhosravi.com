## Infinite accuracy

I want to explain necessity with a simple example.

![Video game sniper scope frame with red labels pointing at the range finder and the bullet drop](https://rezakhosravi.com/img/essays/necessity/01.webp)

*bullet calculation in a video game*

Imagine shooting a bullet in a video game. The computer performs underlying calculations to determine the bullet’s trajectory. However, since man-made calculations are limited by computational power, the result is typically rounded to a few decimal places, perhaps four or more. This introduces a degree of inaccuracy, a result of the limitations of human technology.

Now, let’s consider shooting the same bullet in reality. Unlike in a video game, the real world accounts for every factor: **wind resistance**, **gravity**, air pressure, and so on. These factors are not simplified or rounded; instead, nature calculates the trajectory with **infinite accuracy**. The bullet’s path is determined by the laws of nature, taking into account every detail down to the smallest force or influence.

While we might experience **uncertainty** when trying to predict where the bullet will land, this uncertainty is due to **our lack of knowledge**, not any randomness in nature. Nature, or **God in Spinoza’s terms**, ensures that the bullet flies and lands with perfect accuracy. It could not happen any other way. Everything in nature follows this same principle of **infinite precision**.

For example the point that bullet end up in the space within the game if we say to be the coordinates [10.23][20.56], we see that they are finite. The bullet that we shoot in nature, the calculations of it will happen infinitely accurate as [10.2381239812038...][20.56891273918723...]. This is what is called infinite necessity or infinite accuracy.

### Everything from the falling of an apple to the movement of planets

In any event, from the fall of any object to the movements of your bicycle and planets, God is executing all with infinite accuracy and it makes no exception among its creations. Of course, each area of science has its own laws, what we know by study and experiment, is that those laws are all infinitely deterministic, with no gap for possibility.

### Flexibility of laws of nature

This is the point of Spinoza’s philosophy that science couldn’t disprove.

The infinite accuracy of these laws makes them infinitely inflexible.

If we can flex or change the way a law of nature works, we have found a miracle.

> If we were more conscious of the inflexible universal laws that govern even the wildest and most wanton fantasy, we might perhaps be in a better position to see these thoughts above all others as objective occurrences, just as we see dreams, which nobody is supposed to be deliberate or arbitrary inventions. Carl.Jung: Two Essays in Analytical Psychology, page 276

Here Jung is talking about the efficiency and reality of **active imagination (his technique)**, how inflexible laws of nature make our imaginations necessarily have a source of existence in us, and how the unconscious mind can manifest itself through the dream, daydream, and active made-up imagination.

## Necessity

Necessity of cause and effect is the way that Spinoza proved his concept.

Any event/effect in the world has a cause, otherwise, the effect cannot follow from nothing. The event always follows from its cause or causes. Now with that said, if we consider any event in the world, it has causes which made that event happen, those causes had their own causes, which made them happen, and so on to the Big Bang. So we reach a strict chain of action and reaction and the only way to change something in it is to change the initial conditions (the Big Bang).

![Diagram branching from a circle marked the beginning of God through nodes A, B and C along a time arrow](https://rezakhosravi.com/img/essays/necessity/02.png)

So we can see how everything is brought to reality, by a cause, so hence it follows nothing happens without a reason, which we call **truth**. This is also the reason truth cannot be **judged**: it’s neither good nor bad.

> [>[1P29]](https://ethica.bc.edu/#/element/1P29):Nothing in the universe is contingent, but all things are conditioned to exist and operate in a particular manner by the necessity of the divine nature.

> [>[1P33]:](https://ethica.bc.edu/#/element/1P33) Things could not have been brought into being by God in any manner or in any order different from that which has in fact obtained.

Our mind by default imagines things happening differently, but hence it follows that this only happens in **mind** and **imagination**.

I don’t claim imagination has no effect in reality, actually the opposite. All man-made things we see used to be ideas one day, which were brought to reality.

However, the imagination and action; all followed therefrom, are all products of infinite accuracy.

> [>[2P44.Corollary]](https://ethica.bc.edu/#/element/2P44): Hence it follows, that it is only through our imagination that we consider things, whether in respect to the future or the past, as contingent.

## Chaotic system

> 1972, [Edward Norton Lorenz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Norton_Lorenz): He discovered the effect when he observed runs of his weather model with initial condition data that were rounded in a seemingly inconsequential manner. He noted that the weather model would fail to reproduce the results of runs with the unrounded initial condition data. A very small change in initial conditions had created a significantly different outcome

![Toppling line of dominoes winding across a patterned world under a cosmic swirl, a butterfly above](https://rezakhosravi.com/img/essays/necessity/03.webp)

Weather is a form of **deterministic** [**chaotic system**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butterfly_effect), which means that it is predictable in the short term but not in the long term. Small unpredictable changes completely change what we forecast almost all the time. This happens because of a lack of knowledge, many times inevitable.

The underlying reason for such phenomena is the infinitely accurate laws of nature which make everything possible.

In our everyday lives, we can see how a small change in our actions can lead to a cause that pushes a new set of dominoes that we can’t predict the outcome of.

> > From the necessity of the divine nature must follow infinite things in infinite ways, executed by infinite accuracy, inside the absolutely perfect God. Reza Khosravivala
>
> > Hence it follows that God has (infinite \* infinite) computation power with constant latency in respect to time. Reza Khosravivala

Proof: God makes infinite things possible, in infinite ways. Each of them separately takes the infinite accuracy into account. Therefore it follows that God has (infinite \* infinite) computation power with constant latency with respect to equality of execution or execution latency elapsing.
