About

I started building software in Tehran: backends for a tourism platform, then an e-commerce marketplace. Alongside that I studied psychology at Islamic Azad University and finished the coursework through the final year before I moved to the Netherlands. I've never felt they were separate interests. Both are about finding patterns in complex systems, one made of services and the other of people.

In the Netherlands I spent three and a half years at NN Group building banking systems: payment flows with sub-second fraud checks, compliance services for De Nederlandsche Bank, and the platform of services behind the NN app. Regulated software teaches you to respect evidence, audit trails, and what happens at 3 a.m. when something breaks. Since May 2026 I've been a Site Reliability Engineer at Booking.com.

Short bio (for press & events)

Reza Khosravivala is a software engineer and the founder of Intuita, an AI assistant for mental-health clinicians. He works as a Site Reliability Engineer at Booking.com, and before that built payments, fraud-detection and regulatory-compliance systems at NN Group. He studied psychology at Islamic Azad University, focusing on interpersonal and relational patterns. He writes about clinical AI, mental-health care and building software, and lives in the Netherlands.
